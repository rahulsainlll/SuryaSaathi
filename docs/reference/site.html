The hidden problem

Three causes. One bill that can't tell them apart.

The vendor's easy answer is always "it's just the pollution." But each cause leaves its own signature in the gap between what the roof should make and what it does.

Smog

Nobody's problem

Expected and actual fall together, and recover together.

No action needed

Dust

The vendor's problem

The gap widens every dry day; rain resets it.

Free cleaning, owed for 5 years

Fault

The vendor's problem

Output collapses overnight while sunlight doesn't.

Free repair, owed for 5 years

How it works

From a reading on a roof to a fixed panel.

Scroll to follow one reading from a 3 kW roof in Dwarka, through the diagnosis, to the family's single WhatsApp message.

  1. 1

    Every roof reports itself

    A DISCOM data logger, the inverter's cloud or a camera clip sends yesterday's reading to AWS IoT Core. The family does nothing.

  2. 2

    Compared with what the sun allowed

    Satellite sunlight and haze for that exact roof and day give the output it should have made.

  3. 3

    Smog, dust or fault, told apart

    Smog drops both lines together. Dust widens the gap every dry day. A fault collapses output overnight.

  4. 4

    Claimed, chased, verified

    A free-maintenance claim goes to the vendor with evidence. Miss the deadline and the DISCOM steps in. When output recovers, the family hears once.

Why each service

Every service does one job

AWS IoT Core

Per-device certificates; scales to millions of devices; routes messages without servers.

Amazon Bedrock

Agent reasoning, reading screen photos, and messages in Hindi and English.

AWS Step Functions

Waits days for a vendor at near-zero cost, with a visual audit trail per case.

Amazon DynamoDB

Millisecond lookups by system ID at any scale; old raw readings age out.

Amazon EventBridge

Daily expected-output runs, connector pulls and nightly rollups.

End User Messaging Social

WhatsApp in and out, connected directly to AWS.

Hardware

A camera clip for roofs with nothing

An ESP32-CAM on FreeRTOS, clipped by the vendor in front of the inverter, photographs the screen every hour. Bedrock reads the numbers. It works with any brand and never touches the wiring.

Why now

The duty exists. The tool doesn't.

Delhi's Solar Energy Policy, Second Amendment (17 September 2026), puts monitoring on the DISCOMs. SuryaSaathi is how they do it.

New DISCOM duty

Find under-performing roofs

Monitoring generation and identifying under-performing systems is now the DISCOM's job.

DSAIN

An ID for every roof

Each system gets a Digital Solar Asset Identification Number. SuryaSaathi keeps its generation record accurate.

How we estimate

A 3 kW roof in Delhi makes about 4,700 kWh a year. Recovering 15–18% lost to unchecked dust and faults is 700–850 kWh, or ₹5–6k at ₹7 per kWh.

14.8 GW of rooftop solar makes roughly 20 TWh a year; recovering a tenth of it is about 2 TWh.
